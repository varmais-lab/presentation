Rollout

Pilot, productize, scale.

01
Weeks 1–6
Pilot & signal

Launch 3 flagship series — one per pillar. Seed 30–40 topics mined from existing comment sections. Goal isn't scale, it's finding the 5–6 formats that actually retain.

02
Weeks 6–12
Productize winners

Turn top formats into franchised templates: ₹50k Business Challenge, Degree → Digital, SHG Success. Fixed skeleton, swappable topic, infinite episodes.

03
Month 3+
Scale & distribute

Ramp to a 400–600 video/month vertical, split ~45/30/25 across segments — pushed across all 550+ properties with state-aware framing.
