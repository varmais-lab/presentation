The universal cuff fits upper-arm circumferences from 22 cm to 42 cm, covering most adults. It isn't meant for infants or children.

Two ways: a USB Type-C cable or AA batteries. Handy during power cuts or travel. Check the listing for whether the adapter is included.

Yes. It keeps separate memory for two users — up to 90 readings each — and can show the average of your last three readings.
